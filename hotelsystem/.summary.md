# Hotel Payment & Billing Platform

The hotel payment & billing platform I've designed and built, distilled into a single diagram.

### What's Inside

- **WinForms Desktop Client** — A deliberate choice for team familiarity and long-term maintainability.
- **Card Authorization** — Terminal-based and server-direct card authorization, routed by provider.
- **PMS Integration** — Four PMS integration methods, configurable per hotel.
- **VAN/PG Integration** — Multiple VAN/PG providers through a common plugin interface.
- **Settlement & Billing** — EDI settlement, reconciliation, and hotel billing integration.
- **Database Security** — Role-separated database accounts with restricted access to cardholder data.
- **AI Agent Integration** — An MCP interface for AI agents, governed by the same authentication and least-privilege access controls.
- **Plugin Architecture** — Signed, independently deployed plugins on a shared codebase for both on-premises and cloud deployments.
