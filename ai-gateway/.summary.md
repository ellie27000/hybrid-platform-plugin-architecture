# AI Platform

The AI platform I designed and built for hotel operations.

It's a Spring Boot modular monolith that connects to a hotel payment & billing platform over MCP, and runs on AWS EKS. It supports multiple hotels with tenant-aware authentication, access control, and auditing.

Core implementation and testing are complete, including chat, image-based chat, and document Q&A. Next, the focus shifts to specific workflows, production hardening, and performance tuning.
